# Team Plan

## Scope (state this on the site too)

**In scope:** training-time poisoning of supervised models: availability (indiscriminate), targeted, and backdoor attacks; defenses (detection/sanitization, robust training, model inspection/repair, certification). Frontier: LLM pretraining and instruction tuning, web-scale data, RAG.

**Out of scope:** inference-time adversarial examples, model extraction, privacy attacks, general LLM jailbreaks.
