How this site is organized

The framework below groups attacks by goal, mechanism, and setting, and defenses into four families. Each paper in the list is tagged with its category, and you can filter by it. Software and datasets are listed at the end.

Conceptual framework

Attacks

Goal
Indiscriminate, targeted, backdoor
Mechanism
Label flipping, clean-label, bilevel optimization, trigger insertion
Setting
Classic ML, deep learning, fine-tuning, LLMs, RAG

Defenses

Sanitization
Find and remove poisoned samples
Robust training
Train a clean model on poisoned data
Inspection and repair
Detect or remove a backdoor after training
Certification
Provable limits on poison impact
